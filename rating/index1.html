Great Reviews

  • Tested Great Reviews often test the products in person.
  • Curated Great Reviews always curate and often rank the products.
  • Compared Great Reviews often compare the products against the other listed ones in tables and in the descriptions. They sometimes test rigorously against shared test criteria.
  • Comprehensive Great Reviews often provide extensive and detailed product descriptions.
  • Methodical Great Reviews often state the quality criteria of their products.
  • Objective Great Reviews are always objective and apply their criteria consistently.
  • Illustrated Great Reviews are often illustrated with custom images.
  • Expertise Great Reviews often state the authors' expertise, the external experts they consulted, and they provide external resources.

Decent Reviews

  • Tested Decent Reviews rarely test the products in person.
  • Curated Decent Reviews highlight the good products and explain, why others are worse. They sometimes rank the products.
  • Compared Decent Reviews sometimes compare the products in the descriptions and often provide tables of specifications.
  • Comprehensive Decent Reviews often provide great product descriptions.
  • Methodical Decent Reviews often state good and bad features.
  • Objective Decent Reviews are objective and review according to their criteria.
  • Illustrated Decent Reviews sometimes use non-promotional, non-stock pictures.
  • Expertise Decent Reviews often state the authors expertise and sometimes provide external sources.

Poor Reviews

  • Tested Poor Reviews never test the products in person.
  • Curated Poor Reviews rarely curate products. They only know winners ('best in ...').
  • Compared Poor Reviews sometimes provide tables of specifications but rarely more.
  • Comprehensive Poor Reviews often provide only marketing text and specifications as descriptions.
  • Methodical Poor Reviews often provide brief and shallow pro-con-tables. They sometimes state very general quality criteria.
  • Objective Poor Reviews are rarely objective. What is good for one product may not matter for another one.
  • Illustrated Poor Reviews only use promotional and stock pictures.
  • Expertise Poor Reviews rarely state the expertise used. Sometimes, a broad statement (e.g. 'tech journalist') is made.

Spam Reviews

  • Tested Spam Reviews never test the products.
  • Curated Spam Reviews rarely curate or order products. Often, the Products are enumerated.
  • Compared Spam Reviews rarely compare products.
  • Comprehensive Spam Reviews often provide only specifications or marketing text as descriptions.
  • Methodical Spam Reviews rarely state a methodology for selection or curation.
  • Objective Spam Reviews rarely have objective rating criteria.
  • Illustrated Spam Reviews only use promotional (sales) pictures.
  • Expertise Spam Reviews rarely reference expertise. They sometimes (wrongly) claim expertise.

Webshops

  • Shop pages that list offered items.
  • Manufacturer pages that also offer products.
  • Product search engines that show meta-reviews of price comparisons.

Buyer's Guides

  • The page explains the product category. No individual products are presented!
  • Nothing can be purchased.

Other Websites

  • This page is neither Review, Shop, or Guide.
  • YouTube, Pinterest, Manufacturer pages that are not shops, ...

Ad or Banner Overlays

  • An overlay (Ads, Cookie-Banner, Newsletter) hides most of the page.
  • If the overlay is not disruptive, you can ignore it.

Error Message:

Missing Images or Broken Design

  • The style or design is visibly broken.
  • Most images are missing.
